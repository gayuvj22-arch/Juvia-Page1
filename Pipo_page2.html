Pipo the parrot: how Juvia’s mascot has been used in recent marketing

From late-night phone messages to a “lost parrot” campaign, a look at three ways the juice brand’s cartoon character has reached consumers

MI
Meera IyerConsumer Affairs Correspondent
Published: 19 October 2026, 07:30 IST
Updated: 19 October 2026, 09:12 IST
A−A+4 min read

The ‘lost’ parrot

On 4 October, Pipo told his followers on social media that he was lost and asked them to help find him. The post, tagged #FindPipo, was shared more than 12,000 times, and thousands of people sent him messages.

Ten days later, Pipo said he had been found inside new Juvia packs, along with a contest to win prizes. Pipo’s “lost” story had been planned as a sales campaign from the start.

To enter the contest, buyers had to use a code printed inside the new packs. The contest closes at the end of October.
